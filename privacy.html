Privacy Policy – WhyFlask Insights

Last updated: 20 November 2026

WhyFlask Insights is a private tool used only by the owner of the WhyFlask YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data it reads

With the owner's permission (Google scope yt-analytics.readonly), it reads YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's own channel: views, watch time, retention, traffic sources and subscriber counts. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything.

How the data is used

The numbers are used only to measure how the channel's own videos perform and to plan new videos. They are stored privately on the owner's computer and are not sold, shared or sent to third parties.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the WhyFlask YouTube channel: youtube.com/@whyflask
